Why Brighton

Room to grow
without leaving the good schools behind.

Brighton is the place I send first-time buyers and growing families when they want a yard, a garage, and a 27J classroom — without giving up an easy run to downtown Denver.

  • Family-first 27J School District Strong elementary-through-high pipeline with newer K-8 builds in Brighton Crossing and Prairie View.
  • New construction inventory Active builders in Brighton Crossing, Aspen Ridge, and Sugar Mill — competitive incentives in 2026.
  • Riverdale Regional Park & Barr Lake Two of the metro’s underrated outdoor spots, both in Brighton’s backyard.
  • Direct shot to E-470 & I-76 Twenty minutes to DIA, thirty to downtown — without the I-25 headache.
