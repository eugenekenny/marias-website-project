Why Westminster

A grown-up suburb
with a real downtown
arriving in pieces.

Westminster has the mature trees and mountain views that Thornton and Brighton are still growing into — plus a downtown district that’s been quietly turning into the real thing.

  • Two school districts to choose from Westminster Public and Jefferson County R-1 both serve the city — it matters which side of the street you’re on.
  • Downtown Westminster is real now Walkable mixed-use core with new restaurants, the library, and Alamo Drafthouse anchoring the neighborhood.
  • Standley Lake & Westminster Hills Open space, off-leash trails, and one of the best sunset views of the Front Range you’ll find inside city limits.
  • 20 minutes to downtown Denver US-36 corridor · Flatiron Flyer bus express straight to Union Station.
