Why Arvada

A small-town main street
with foothill views
and a light-rail stop.

Arvada is the answer when buyers tell me they want “somewhere walkable, with character, but not Denver.” Olde Town is its own thing, and the rest of the city quietly knows it.

  • Olde Town Arvada is the magnet Walkable historic core with award-winning restaurants, the new G Line light rail, and a Saturday farmer’s market with a queue.
  • Jefferson County R-1 schools Five Star K–8 schools across the city, with Ralston Valley and Pomona running competitive high school programs.
  • Foothill access in 15 minutes North Table Mountain, Standley Lake, and the Front Range trailheads are weekend-easy.
  • 20 minutes to downtown G Line light rail · I-70 · Wadsworth Boulevard — three good options depending on the day.
